INTRODUCTION
TTTHEN  the  Spaniards  discovered  the  Philippines ^  ^  in  the  sixteenth  century,  they  found  the  tribes along  the  coasts  of  |he  different  islands  already  some- what influenced  by  trade  with  China,  Siam,  and  the islands  to  the  south.

Then,  no  longer  being  at war  with  one  another,  they  made  great  advances  in civilization,  while  the  hill  tribes  have  remained  iso- lated, retaining  their  old  customs  and  beliefs.
The  tales  of  the  Christianized  tribes  include  a  great mixture  of  old  ideas  and  foreign  influences  obtained through  contact  with  the  outside  world.
