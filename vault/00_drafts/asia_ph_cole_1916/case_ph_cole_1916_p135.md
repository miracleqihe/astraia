Bagobo  (Mindanao)
SOON  after  people  were  created  on  the  earth,  there was  born  a  child  named  Lumabet,  who  lived  to  be a  very,  very  old  man.  He  could  talk  when  he  was but  one  day  old,  and  all  his  life  he  did  wonderful things  until  the  people  came  to  believe  that  he  had been  sent  by  Manama,  the  Great  Spirit.
When  Lumabet  was  still  a  young  man  he  had  a fine  dog,  and  he  enjoyed  nothing  so  much  as  taking him  to  the  mountains  to  hunt.  One  day  the  dog  no- ticed a  white  deer.  Lumabet  and  his  companions started  in  pursuit,  but  the  deer  was  very  swift  and they  could  not  catch  it.  On  and  on  they  went  until they  had  gone  around  the  world,  and  still  the  deer was  ahead.  One  by  one  his  companions  dropped  out of  the  chase,  but  Lumabet  would  not  give  up  until  he had  the  deer.
